**Closure date:** 2026-09-01

## Claim being closed

Refunds and reversals are no longer treated as permanently zero-effect or ordinary income. Their economic projection is relationship-aware and bounded by the contribution of the event they offset.

Current rule:

```text
unlinked refund/reversal
→ no automatic economic mutation

linked partial refund
→ negative expense delta up to remaining original expense

linked full refund
→ can reduce net expense to zero

cumulative offset > remaining contribution
→ REQUIRES_REVIEW

exact linked reversal
→ negates original contribution

reversal after prior partial offset / incompatible amount
→ REQUIRES_REVIEW
```

## Explicit non-claims

This receipt does not prove automatic relationship discovery across all real providers. It closes the projection semantics once a relationship is established or remains unresolved.
