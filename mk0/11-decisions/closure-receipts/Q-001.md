## Claim being closed

The MK0 canonical resolver has an explicit, internally consistent semantic model for observed financial movements covered by the current scope:

```text
movement semantics
+ flow direction
+ ownership/relationship
+ event relationship
→ economic effect
→ projection state
```

The model does not infer economic meaning from debit/credit direction or transfer mechanism alone.

## Closure criteria audit

### Semantic corpus

`spikes/canonical-resolver/fixtures/classification-cases.json` contains 54 bounded semantic cases and remains green.

### Internal transfer

Tenant-owned transfers contribute zero income and zero expense.

### Card settlement

Credit-card settlement is neutral when purchases are already represented, preventing double counting.

### External transfer

External movement starts unresolved and cannot silently mutate totals. Explicit resolved effects must be direction-compatible.

### Refund

Unlinked refund remains unresolved; linked refunds offset the original expense and cumulative automatic offsets cannot exceed the remaining original contribution.

### Reversal

Unlinked reversal remains unresolved; exact compatible linked reversal negates the original economic contribution. Prior offsets or incompatible amounts require review.

### Unknown movement

Unknown semantics never fabricate an effect. An explicit compatible economic resolution is required.

### Contradictions

`C-001` and `C-002` have explicit closure receipts and revalidation triggers.
