```text
provider/source identifier != canonical economic identity
```

Automatic identity requires stronger evidence than amount/merchant/time similarity, and hard contradictions override strong references.

## Identity rules proven in the bounded spike

### Exact replay

Same stable evidence identity does not increase canonical event count.

### Strong-link merge

Order, receipt, invoice, authorization or provider-transaction references can support automatic merge when no hard contradiction exists.

### Weak similarity

Same amount/merchant/time/source-family similarity cannot silently prove identity. Strong cross-source similarity without hard linkage routes to review.

### Hard contradiction gates

Known mismatch on any current gate prevents automatic identity:

```text
tenant
currency
amount at cent precision
flow direction
financial account when both known
payment instrument when both known
incompatible semantic type
```

### False-merge preference

Two genuine same-source/same-value purchases remain separate. The design intentionally prefers a recoverable false split/review over a silent false merge.

### Multi-device replay

`spikes/e2ee-sync/test/lease-failure.test.js` demonstrates that duplicate processing work after lease failure does not make the coordination lease the sole correctness mechanism.
