**Title:** External transfer is not an economic category  
**Closure date:** 2026-09-01

## Claim being closed

FinanceSensor now separates movement mechanism/direction from economic effect.

An external transfer does not automatically become income, expense or neutral. Without explicit economic meaning evidence, it remains `REQUIRES_REVIEW` and contributes zero to authoritative income/expense totals until resolved.

When explicitly resolved, direction and economic effect must be compatible:

```text
OUT + EXPENSE  → allowed
IN  + INCOME   → allowed
ANY + NEUTRAL  → allowed when explicitly established
OUT + INCOME   → review
IN  + EXPENSE  → review
```

## Explicit non-claims

This receipt does not prove automatic purpose inference for every transfer, nor real-provider classification accuracy. It closes the **semantic rule**, not every future classifier.
