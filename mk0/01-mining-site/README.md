## Source quality

Use the registry in [`../../research/SOURCES.md`](../../research/SOURCES.md).

Preference order:

1. regulator / official platform policy;
2. official engineering documentation;
3. regulatory or financial filings;
4. official product documentation;
5. reputable independent reporting;
6. community reports as anecdotal failure signals.

No architectural decision may cite a community report as if it were authoritative platform behavior.
