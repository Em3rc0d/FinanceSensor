# Q-006 — Sustainable Monetization

**Priority:** P0  
**Status:** OPEN

## Question

What business model can sustain FinanceSensor without creating incentives that conflict with the user's financial interests?

## Evidence pattern

The competitive mining shows two useful signals:

- paid products such as YNAB and Monarch demonstrate that consumers can pay directly for trustworthy financial software;
- products such as Money Dashboard and Yolt demonstrate that adoption alone does not guarantee sustainable economics.

References live in [`../../research/SOURCES.md`](../../research/SOURCES.md).

## Models to evaluate

- direct subscription;
- freemium with clearly bounded premium intelligence;
- household plan;
- paid advanced connectors;
- B2B2C/private-label later;
- optional partner offers with strict conflict-of-interest controls.

## Anti-pattern

Do not make FinanceSensor's intelligence optimize for affiliate conversion while presenting itself as neutral financial guidance.

If partner recommendations ever exist, the product must distinguish:

```text
user-benefit recommendation
vs
sponsored/partner offer
```

## MK0 relevance

No billing system is required for MK0, but the architecture and product thesis should avoid assumptions that require selling financial data or advertising against sensitive behavior.

## Closure criteria

- candidate unit economics modeled;
- privacy implications compared by model;
- conflict-of-interest policy drafted;
- free/premium boundary hypothesis documented;
- explicit decision recorded before public commercial launch.
