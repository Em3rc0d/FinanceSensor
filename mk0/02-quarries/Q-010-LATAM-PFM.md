# Q-010 — Latin America PFM / Open Finance Landscape

**Priority:** P1  
**Status:** OPEN

## Question

What does the LatAm market imply for FinanceSensor's source strategy, language, categories and commercialization?

## Initial evidence

The competitive mining includes Guiabolso/PicPay, Olivia/Nubank, Finerio Connect and Spanish-language Fintonic. These provide evidence that automated PFM and financial-intelligence products have had meaningful adoption and strategic value in Spanish/Portuguese markets.

See [`../../research/MINING-001-COMPETITIVE-ARCHAEOLOGY.md`](../../research/MINING-001-COMPETITIVE-ARCHAEOLOGY.md).

## Research dimensions

- Open Finance maturity by country;
- bank aggregator coverage;
- email receipt/bank-notification conventions;
- local wallet/payment systems;
- cash prevalence and resulting coverage limitations;
- common card/account structures;
- currencies and FX;
- Spanish/Portuguese merchant normalization;
- privacy/regulatory requirements by launch country;
- willingness to pay and pricing sensitivity.

## Peru-first examples worth mining later

- bank purchase-notification emails;
- credit-card statements and memberships/fees;
- Yape/Plin-like wallet evidence where available through permitted sources;
- SUNAT electronic receipt/invoice artifacts only where legally and technically appropriate.

These are research targets, not current connector commitments.

## Closure criteria

- launch-country matrix created;
- Peru source landscape documented first;
- regional connector providers compared;
- cash/coverage limitation explicitly modeled in product copy;
- category/merchant localization requirements documented;
- no assumption that U.S. banking connectivity patterns transfer unchanged to LatAm.
