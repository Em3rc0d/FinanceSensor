## Source-quality policy

1. Official vendor documentation and regulators outrank marketing pages.
2. SEC/regulatory filings are preferred for acquisition/user-count/business claims when available.
3. Company marketing metrics are recorded as company-reported, not independently verified facts.
4. Reddit/community reports may reveal failure modes, but are anecdotal and require technical validation.
5. Every quarry must separate **evidence**, **finding**, **implication**, and **decision**.
